## W-1: what was built (existing art only)
- **Layout:** `blueprint_wide.txt` on the 64×40 inner grid, laid over the farm's inner area at its real world position, with a 6-tile forest margin round it. The live farm underneath is hidden in the tool only:
  - main's own drawing and its standing layers are made transparent;
  - the live house, cave, roost and desk lights are hidden and moved off-screen, so they take no share of the per-item light limit.
- **Camera:** the game's own, following the farmer. `SCALE` 3, 1280×720, unchanged.
- **Ground, batched:**
  - The static ground is drawn once into 16×16-tile chunk images, 20 chunks including the margin, shown as one `Sprite2D` each. It includes the PixelLab path sets (two mixed for edge variety), worn stone round the well and in wide paths, leaf litter in drifts, decals and ground cover gathered at edges, and the production `ground_water` for the pond.
  - The chunks are unlit, so the night tint and every `PointLight2D` light them at run time. Checked: the windows, porch and lamp posts light the ground; so does the lantern (see the lantern finding below).
  - Not baked: no light, shadow or anything dynamic. The forest's depth shade is baked as a darker albedo, the same strength as R3-1's forest shade.
- **Standing things:** each is its own sprite, sorted every frame by its base against the farmer's feet, so walking behind and in front works. That covers the trees, the oak and its boulders, fungus and charms, the house, the well, fences, beds, crops, corn, trellis and props.
- **Assets used:**
  - Production (SpriteCook): `house_stage_3`, `prop_well`, `woodstore`, `crop_pumpkin/turnip/corn` (ripe frames), `ground_water`, `weather_fog`.
  - ART-003 (PixelLab): oak D, boulders, fungus, charms; path, path2, wear and litter sets; decals, ground cover; fir, maple, undergrowth, lantern post, fence; garden bed, crops, crops_b, garden props; log.
  - No fruit tree or dock art exists: maples stand in for the orchard, and the dock is plain path.
- **Lights at night:**
  - the farmer's lantern;
  - the two lantern posts (stand-ins for earned lamps, as in the slice);
  - the restored house's own two window lights and porch light, placed where the production game puts them on this sprite.
  - No other light was added; at most 6 are on in any view.
- **Views** (farmer at, inner tiles): V1 (37.5, 9.5); V2 (49, 24); V3 (31, 19); V4 (14, 27.5); V5 (45, 12.5). V1-B: (38.5, 10) on layout B.

## W-2: results

### The five views (judgement, from the sheets)
- **V1, homestead core:** the oak, the house with its door, porch and lit windows, and the well all share one screen, day and night. This is the closest the game camera gets to concept 02's heart.
- **V2, garden:** the whole fenced garden fits one screen: four raised beds in a 2×2 grid, walkways, corn and the bean trellis along the north fence, both gates. The dirt floor dominates and the beds look small in it.
  - The two east beds use the production crop strips (pumpkins, turnips, corn); they read far brighter than the PixelLab crops in the west beds, day and night.
- **V3, arrival:** the well, two lamp-lit paths, the oak's roots and the orchard's corner. The weakest view: mostly open grass and path, with little to look at.
- **V4, orchard and pond:** the maples behind their fence read as an orchard. The pond is the production water: a flat, blocky, dark teal shape with hard grid edges that doesn't match the PixelLab ground, and it has no reeds, lily pads or dock art.
- **V5, porch:** the house front, the dressing (firewood, barrels, crates, flowers), the well and the oak's crown. Strong at night with the window and porch light.

### Continuity: walking between the views
- **The route:** porch → well → oak → back past the well → garden west gate → walkway → south gate → road → pond dock. 23.6 s at 30 fps (`walk_day.gif`, `walk_night.gif`; 12-frame grids in master part 4). The farmer finishes it day and night with no stalls.
- **Paths and sightlines:** the well is in V1, V3 and V5, and the garden fence and corn show from the well. The road joins the garden to the pond; the porch path leads straight to the well. No leg crosses an empty, unconnected screen.
- **Weak link:** the stretch from V3 to the road and along it is mostly grass and path, so the place feels thinner there than at V1, V2 or V5.

### Layout problems found and fixed during W-2 (prototype only)
1. **Garden south gate one tile wide.** The farmer's feet box (30 of 48 units) only passes a one-tile gap when lined up within ±9 units; the first walks stalled there. **Fixed:** the gate is now two tiles wide, and the walker steers to 0.08 tile. Recorded: one-tile gates are fiddly in play too.
2. **Tall firs just south of open ground.** Their crowns covered the south road and the farmer on it, and stood in front of the pond (first walk). **Fixed:** forest tiles with open ground within three tiles to the north get low undergrowth instead of tall trees. Re-rendered every prototype view and both walks after the fix.

### Other findings
- **Lantern on grass.** Under the R3-1 night the lantern pool is much weaker on dark grass than on the dirt path: 0.16–0.18 on grass against 0.235 on the path at the same night, and R3-1's 0.325. Every R3-1 measurement had the farmer on the path. Raising its energy would be a lighting change (P-1 territory), so this is recorded, not changed.
- **Style mismatch (W-3, a comparison confound):** the production SpriteCook house, well, water and crops sit beside PixelLab ground and trees. The house and well read cleaner, with bolder outlines; the water reads flat.
- **Fences:** north–south runs draw as posts only (no rail art exists for that direction). Gates are gaps.
- **Litter:** drifts are concentrated now, under the oak and at the forest and orchard edges, not spread evenly. The drift under the oak is still a large orange patch.
- **Performance per screen:** see the table. The baked ground keeps draw calls within about 40–110 of production at the same spot (678–808 against 635–708), where the unbaked slice needed 1,084. Frame time median is 2.4–3.3 ms against production's 1.9–2.8 ms. The extra is mostly the tool's per-frame sort of about 1,500 sprites in GDScript, which a production version wouldn't do the same way.
- **Light limit:** at most 6 lights on in any view; the hidden production lights are kept off-screen. Not a constraint here.

## Suggested decisions (for the owner; not decisions)
1. V1 framing: B or A.
2. W-3 art priorities if authorized: the pond edge and dock first (the largest mismatch), then oak D's canopy, then brighter crops, then a fruit tree.
3. Whether the production house stays as the comparison house, or a prototype-family house is planned (W-3).
4. Dressing for V3 and the road (stones, a cart, a scarecrow, a lamp-post stand-in) in W-4, so the spaces between vignettes don't feel empty.
5. Whether the lantern's weakness on grass should be part of any later P-1 proposal.
