# ART-003 W-4 Task A: woodpile, orchard, closed east gate and forest margin (non-production)

Status: for ChatGPT's and the owner's review. **Prototype only** (`art=w4` in `tools/art003_wide.gd`). The owner authorized it in the W-4 brief, after ChatGPT's review `6595947`.

- **Not changed:** the live farm, gameplay, saves, camera, scale, `DESIGN.md`, production art, P-1, and stages 0–2.
- **Generation:** PixelLab 0 credits, SpriteCook 0. Every new pixel is hand-placed by script from colours already in the prototype art. No asset IDs to record.
- **Baseline:** Batch C (`art=w3c`) is the BEFORE in every comparison. `art=w4` adds only the changes below.
- **Renders:**
  - BEFORE is a `git archive` export of `6595947`, run with `art=w3c`.
  - AFTER is the same export plus the edited `tools/art003_wide.gd` and `w4/`, run with `art=w4`.
  - Neither export has the VIS-002 work.
  - Settings: `layout=b grass=1` (wall A, oak D, G1), 1280×720, 3×, the game's own camera, sunny autumn, sanity 100. Day is 12:00; night is 21:30 under R3-1, lantern tier 1.

## What changed
### 1. Log pile in place of the SpriteCook woodstore (V3 and both house wings)
- `props/logpile.png`, 19×16: two rounds stacked on three, end grain toward the viewer, bark tops behind.
- Drawn pixel by pixel by `src/make_logpile.py`, using only the colours of the PixelLab `r3/log.png`: tan end grain with a pale catch-light, a darker heart ring and a bark rim.
- A first pass in the log's bright yellow read as coins at 4×, so it was muted to the log's own tan tones.
- **Placement:** it stands on the same tiles the woodstore used: V3 (35, 14) and the house dressing tiles (39, 10) and (51, 10). The blocked tiles are unchanged.
- **Size:** it is 1.2 tiles wide where the woodstore was 1 wide and 2 tall. It is now lower than the chopping block's handle, so it reads as part of the woodcutting corner rather than as a cabinet.

### 2. Orchard: crown variation and harvest dressing (no regeneration)
`src/make_orchard.py` edits the two Batch C trees. It recolours pixels, fills notches from their own neighbours and stamps fruit; nothing is resampled.
- **apple A:** the Batch C apple, with one-pixel notches in the crown's outline filled.
- **apple B:** the crown's darkest reds turned olive, so the red apples stand out, plus four more apples. The three-tone crown now reads more like an apple tree than a maple.
- **pear A and pear B:** the Batch C pear, filled, with nine small pears set into the crown in two different arrangements; pear B has a greener crown top.
  - **Fix:** the pears' first rims were near-black and looked like icons, so they were softened to bark brown.
- **Layout:** the six trees use the four variants, flipped or not and a step lighter or darker (0.93 to 1.04), so no two neighbours match. They stand on the same tiles as before.
- **Harvest dressing:**
  - 13 windfalls (3×3 apples and pears) lie flat around the trees' feet. They are walkable, like the lily pads.
  - An 11×8 basket of picked fruit sits in front of the Batch C apple barrel, on the barrel's own blocked tile, so no collision changed.
- **Limits:**
  - The trees are still the Batch C size, about a third of the oak's. "Two sizes" would need new art or resampling; neither was done.
  - The windfalls are small at gameplay size.

### 3. Closed east gate and forest avenue (reversible prototype experiment)
- **Source of the tiles:** read at run time from the approved WH blueprint (`wh/exit_blueprint.txt`, PROPOSED grid): 76 tiles from x 59 east.
  - Gate posts sit at (59, 33) and (59, 36), with closed leaves at (59, 34–35).
  - The avenue runs x 60–77 with verges, bending north-east in the border.
  - The blueprint's `X` posts are held as `Q` inside the tool, so the fence code doesn't draw them as fence.
- **Collision is unchanged by construction:**
  - The gate and avenue are a drawn-only overlay. The farmer's collision (`_blocked`) reads `_code_base`, the base layout, where every one of these tiles is forest.
  - The gate therefore blocks exactly as the forest did, and the avenue can't be entered.
  - The player's own clamp (`player.max_pos`) also stops at the inner farm's edge.
- **Art** (`src/make_gate.py`, the fence's colours and its iron):
  - two capped gateposts, 10×34;
  - two plank leaves, 8×24: the north leaf carries an iron latch, the south leaf a hinge strap;
  - a fence spur into the trees on each side, drawn only;
  - a soft shadow line across the road at the leaves' foot.
- **The avenue's forest:**
  - North of the cut, every forest tile carries a fir, standing on deep-forest shade. Before this fix it was the light, sparse edge of the farm's forest, and the cut read as a clearing.
  - Tile depth for the shade ignores the overlay, so the forest round the avenue stays as dark as before.
  - The avenue ground itself darkens under the canopy from x 60 to x 71.
- **No light was added.** Night still has 6 lights on.

### 4. Forest margin 6 → 14 tiles
- `MARGIN` is 14 for `art=w4`, the live border's width (`BORDER_X`); `BORDER_Y` is 8, so 14 covers both. Earlier `art=` values keep 6.
- **Result:** the grey-green strip at the road end is gone. So is the one at every other edge the camera can reach: the WH defect showed at the east end, but the south-east and north-west corners showed it too (see `w4_margin.png`).

## Performance (median frame ms over 3 interleaved runs; draw calls; lights)
**First measurement, before the sort fix:** AFTER was **+0.7 to +1.1 ms** slower in every view, while GPU and render-CPU time stayed flat.
- **The cause:** the tool re-sorts every standing sprite each frame in GDScript. The 14-tile margin adds several hundred forest sprites.
- **The fix:** those sprites never move, so `art=w4` now sorts them once and sorts only the farmer each frame. Earlier `art=` values keep the per-frame loop, so past packages stay comparable.
- **Images unchanged:** captures with and without the fix differ only by the usual run-to-run noise. Two identical BEFORE runs differ by the same amount, from grain and drifting effects.

## Weak points (mine)
- **The gate reads as a column, not a gate.**
  - The cause is the projection, not the drawing: a gate across an east-west road runs north-south, so in the straight-on 3/4 view it is seen end-on, the same way the garden's north-south fences are.
  - Heavier posts, lower leaves, a latch, spurs and the shadow line help, but at gameplay size it still reads as posts across the road rather than as a gate.
  - The fix would be a layout change: bend the road so the gate runs east-west in a north or south opening. That is a blueprint change, so it is an owner decision; not done.
- **The avenue's far bend** (x ≥ 70) is visible only at the camera's last tiles. Mostly it reads as a road running into dark trees, which was the intent at night.
- **The orchard is fuller in fruit read, not in size.** The crowns are still small beside the oak.
- **The windfalls and the basket are tiny** at half size.
- **The log pile at night** is a dark low shape; the end grain doesn't catch the lantern much.
