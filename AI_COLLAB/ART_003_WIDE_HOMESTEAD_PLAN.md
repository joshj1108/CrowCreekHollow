## 5. Expected draw calls and frame time (estimates)

- **Measured basis:** the R3 slice drew 1,084 calls at night in a 1280×720 view (production 606) at 3.0–3.4 ms median on this machine. Draw calls follow what is on screen, so a wider prototype at the slice's density costs about the same per screen.
- **Estimate if built the slice's way** (one sprite per tile, decal and prop): 1,000–1,600 draw calls per screen; 3–5 ms median per frame; about 10,000–15,000 Sprite2D nodes for the whole 64×40 area; a few seconds to build at start-up. Still well inside a 16.7 ms frame. Unmeasured.
- **Proposed instead: bake the ground.** Draw the ground (Wang tiles, wear, litter, cobbles, decals) once into images, one per 16×16-tile chunk (12 chunks for 64×40), and draw those as 12 sprites.
  - The ground becomes about 12 canvas items instead of thousands.
  - Expected: about 500–900 draw calls per screen, close to the R3-1 baseline.
- **Light limit:** each chunk is one canvas item, so each chunk can take 15 lights on screen. Only the lantern, the porch and garden lamps, the house windows and a visitor are expected, which is well under the limit. The R3-0 probe showed that off-screen lights don't count.
- **To measure in implementation:** frame time, draw calls and objects at each of the five views, day and night, against production at the same screen positions.

- **All six landmarks** span 58 × 36 tiles: 2.2 × 2.4 screens, or 5.2 screens of area. **Concept 02's single overview can't be shown in one gameplay frame.**
- **The oak, house and well** together span 27 × 16 tiles, just over one screen (26.67 × 15). They are the only landmark group that fits together, and only from around V1 (house 85%, oak 100%, well 100%).
- **The garden** fits whole in one screen (V2), but nothing else does from there.

**Composition strategies within the locked camera:**
- **Vignettes:** each of V1–V5 is composed as its own picture, with a landmark, a path and a forest edge. This is the concept's richness, delivered one screen at a time.
- **Sightlines:** paths leave each screen pointing at the next landmark. The oak's crown and the house roof are the tallest silhouettes, placed so that one of them is at the edge of most screens (V3, V5).
- **Overlapping vignettes:** the well sits in V1, V3 and V5 and at the edge of V2, so moving between areas never passes through an empty screen.
- **Research options only, not proposed** (each needs its own approved test plan under D-007): a wider canvas or 2× scale (C-02); a presentation-only overview (title, diary map) showing the whole homestead (C-01).
